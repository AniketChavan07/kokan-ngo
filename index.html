About us

Learning changes what a family can hope for

Hope for All is a non-profit that helps children stay in school and helps women build independent livelihoods. We work alongside local communities, so every programme is shaped by the people it serves.

Since our first classroom opened, we have focused on one idea: when a child learns and a woman earns, a whole neighbourhood moves forward.

Our Work

Three programmes, one goal: give people the tools to shape their own future.

Child Education

»

Free tuition, school supplies and mentors that keep children learning and in class.

Women Empowerment

»

Self-help groups, small loans and legal awareness so women can lead at home and at work.

Skill Training

»

Hands-on courses in tailoring, computers and spoken English that lead to real jobs.
